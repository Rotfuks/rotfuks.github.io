Three core jobs.

Everything else is detail.

01 · Evaluate

Quantitative + qualitative.

Every supplement gets two scores: an evidence grade from RCTs, and a real-world rating from people who actually took it. Both visible. No averaging the truth away.

02 · Apply

Three ways in.

Start from a goal and get a plan. Look up a single supplement and see if it earns its place. Check a label and learn how to take it. The product meets you wherever you start.

03 · Partner

Labs + suppliers.

Strategic partnerships with certified labs make Blutbild-as-a-Service possible. Curated supplement providers handle fulfilment — never editorial. The wall between data and commerce stays up.

Why independent

The supplement aisle is not your friend.

Industry-funded research is significantly more likely to favor the sponsor. Most supplement apps are funded by, partnered with, or reselling for the brands they review.

Vital is structurally different — and that's the entire pitch.
